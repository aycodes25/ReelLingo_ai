# 📢 Marketing & Advertising Strategy

ReelLingo solves a very visual and auditory problem. Your marketing shouldn't be text; it should be **"Before and After" videos.**

## 🎯 Target Audience (Who to advertise to)

1. **YouTubers in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya** (Look for channels with 50k - 1M subscribers posting in local languages).
2. **TikTok Comedy Skit Makers** (e.g., Mark Angel Comedy style creators, Taaooma, Sabinus).
3. **Nollywood Indie Filmmakers** on YouTube.
4. **Online Course Creators** selling tech/business courses to Africans.

## 🚀 How to Advertise (Zero to Low Budget)

### 1. The "Before & After" Viral Strategy (Twitter/X & TikTok)
- Take a famous Yoruba/Pidgin skit from a popular creator (e.g., Sabinus).
- Run it through your app to dub it into perfect Spanish or English.
- Post it side-by-side: "What if [Creator] was famous in Mexico? Built an AI that dubs African creators. Takes 5 mins."
- Tag the creator. This almost always goes viral because it's fascinating to watch.

### 2. Direct Outreach (Cold DMing)
- Do not run Facebook ads immediately. Go direct.
- Find 50 mid-tier YouTubers (50k - 200k subs).
- Send them an email/DM: *"Hey [Name], I built an AI tool that translates Yoruba videos to English in 5 mins with your exact voice. I did a 1-minute demo of your last video here: [Link]. I'll give you a free month if you want to try it for your next upload."*
- If the tool works, they will shout you out to their audience.

### 3. Community Marketing
- Join WhatsApp and Telegram groups for African content creators.
- Join Discord servers for African video editors and videographers (they are the ones who usually get asked to add subtitles).
- Pitch it as a tool to *save editors time*.

### 4. Influencer Partnerships
- Find tech reviewers on YouTube/TikTok (e.g., Fisayo Fosudo, Kagotech).
- Give them free lifetime access to make a review video titled: *"How to make money on YouTube globally using this AI tool."*

## 📈 Paid Ads (Once you have revenue)
- **YouTube Pre-roll Ads:** Run short ads before videos related to "How to grow on YouTube" or "Video editing tutorial Premiere Pro."
- **Instagram/Facebook:** Target interests: "Content Creator", "Video Editing", "YouTuber", restricted to Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, and Ghana.
