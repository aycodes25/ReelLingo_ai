# 💰 Monetization Strategy

The goal is to provide exceptional value to creators while maintaining healthy margins. Because ElevenLabs (the dubbing AI) charges per character, we must strictly tie pricing to video length.

## 📦 Subscription Tiers

### 1. Free Tier (Lead Gen)
- **Price:** $0 (₦0)
- **Value:** 1 video per month. Max 3 minutes.
- **Limitations:** ReelLingo watermark on the output video. No voice cloning (uses standard AI voices).
- **Purpose:** Let users experience the magic without paying.

### 2. Starter Tier (For Hobbyists/TikTokers)
- **Price:** $10/month (₦16,000)
- **Value:** Up to 5 videos per month. Max 10 minutes each.
- **Perks:** No watermark. Basic voice cloning included.
- **Margin:** Your cost is roughly $1.50 per 10 min video = $7.50 max cost. Profit: **$2.50/mo minimum**.

### 3. Creator Tier (For YouTubers - The Sweet Spot)
- **Price:** $25/month (₦40,000)
- **Value:** Up to 10 videos per month. Max 25 minutes each.
- **Perks:** Premium voice cloning (sounds exactly like them), priority processing queue, subtitle downloads.
- **Margin:** Very healthy, as most creators won't use all 10 videos every month.

### 4. Studio Tier (For Media & Nollywood)
- **Price:** $80/month (₦130,000)
- **Value:** Up to 30 videos per month. Max 60 minutes each.
- **Perks:** Unlimited languages, highest priority processing, dedicated WhatsApp support.

## 💳 Pay-As-You-Go (One-Off Videos)
Not everyone wants a subscription. Offer a pay-per-video option:
- **$3** for up to 5 minutes.
- **$5** for up to 15 minutes.
- **$10** for up to 30 minutes.
- **$20** for up to 60 minutes.

## 🤝 B2B Enterprise / Agency Model
- Reach out to marketing agencies in Lagos and Nairobi.
- Offer them an "Agency Account" where they buy credits in bulk at a 20% discount. They charge their clients premium rates for localized ads.

## 🛡️ Preventing Losses
- **Strict Hard Caps:** If a user on a Starter plan uploads a 20-minute video, the system must trim it or prompt an upsell.
- **ElevenLabs Voice Reuse:** If a user dubs 5 videos in a month, only clone their voice once, and reuse the cloned model for the other 4 videos to save on cloning overhead.
